# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation free from harassment. We welcome people of every
background, identity, experience level, and ability.

## Expected behavior

Use respectful language. Give specific technical feedback. Accept correction.
Protect private data and credentials. Put project safety before urgency.

## Unacceptable behavior

Do not harass, threaten, insult, stalk, or expose private information. Do not use
sexualized language or unwanted attention. Do not disrupt project spaces.

## Enforcement

Report conduct issues through the repository maintainer contact. Maintainers will
review the report in private. They can correct, warn, suspend, or ban a person.
They will protect reporter privacy where the law permits.

This policy uses the principles of Contributor Covenant 2.1.
